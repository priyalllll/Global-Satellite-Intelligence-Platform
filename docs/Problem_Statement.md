# Problem Statement
The global satellite ecosystem has expanded significantly with the rapid growth of government space programs, commercial satellite operators, and private aerospace organizations. Despite the increasing availability of public satellite data, information remains fragmented across multiple datasets, organizations, and formats, making comprehensive analysis complex, time-consuming, and inconsistent. This fragmentation limits the ability of researchers, analysts, educators, and policy stakeholders to effectively monitor satellite operations, evaluate industry trends, compare national space capabilities, and derive actionable insights. The Global Satellite Intelligence Platform (GSIP) aims to address this challenge by providing a centralized, structured, and interactive analytics platform that consolidates satellite data, supports advanced analytical exploration, and enables evidence-based decision-making through modern data analytics and business intelligence techniques.

## Background

The global satellite ecosystem has expanded significantly due to advancements in government space programs, commercial satellite operators, and private aerospace organizations. The increasing number of satellite launches and missions has generated large volumes of publicly available data related to satellite operations, launch vehicles, mission categories, orbital characteristics, and ownership.

---

## Existing Problem

Although extensive satellite data is publicly available, it is distributed across multiple organizations and datasets with varying formats and levels of detail. This fragmentation makes it difficult to perform comprehensive analysis, compare global satellite ecosystems, identify long-term trends, and generate meaningful insights efficiently.

---

## Business Impact

The absence of a centralized analytical platform increases the effort required to prepare, clean, and analyze satellite data. Researchers, analysts, educators, and policy stakeholders spend significant time consolidating information instead of focusing on data-driven analysis and decision-making.

---

## Proposed Solution

The Global Satellite Intelligence Platform (GSIP) addresses these challenges by integrating publicly available satellite datasets into a centralized analytics platform. Through structured data processing, SQL-based analysis, exploratory data analysis, and interactive Power BI dashboards, the platform enables users to explore the global satellite ecosystem, monitor industry trends, compare satellite operations, and derive reliable insights for informed decision-making.
