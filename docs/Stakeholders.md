# Stakeholder Analysis

## Primary Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Researchers | Analyze satellite trends and conduct research. |
| Data Analysts | Explore satellite datasets and generate insights. |
| Students | Learn data analytics and understand the global satellite ecosystem. |

---

## Secondary Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Educators | Use GSIP as a teaching and demonstration platform. |
| Aerospace Companies | Monitor industry trends and market developments. |
| Government & Space Agencies | Support comparative analysis and strategic planning. |
| Policy Makers | Understand high-level trends in the space sector. |
| Space Enthusiasts | Explore satellite information through interactive dashboards. |

---

## Stakeholder Expectations

- Reliable and accurate satellite data.
- Interactive and user-friendly dashboards.
- Easy comparison of countries, operators, missions, and orbital distributions.
- Actionable insights through data visualization.
- Support for research, learning, and strategic analysis.

---

## Stakeholder Priority

**High Priority**
- Researchers
- Data Analysts
- Students

**Medium Priority**
- Educators
- Aerospace Companies

**Lower Priority**
- Government & Space Agencies
