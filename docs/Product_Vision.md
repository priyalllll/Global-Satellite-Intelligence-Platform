The Global Satellite Intelligence Platform (GSIP) aims to provide a centralized, data-driven analytics platform that transforms fragmented satellite and space industry data into meaningful, actionable insights. By integrating data engineering, analytical processing, and interactive business intelligence, GSIP enables researchers, analysts, educators, and policy stakeholders to explore global satellite operations, monitor industry trends, compare space ecosystems, and support informed decision-making through reliable and accessible visual analytics.
